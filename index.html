Welcome to the King of Colors Tournament

King of Colors is a competitive Risk tournament system created by b_w_johan, featuring a unique ranked structure ranging from a Knight all the way to the King.

Players earn and lose points depending on their rank, game mode, and performance. As ranks rise, the stakes become increasingly brutal. Only one player can claim the throne. Can you be the one to rise through the ranks and become the next King of Colors?
